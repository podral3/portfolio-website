LegalOps Infrastructure

Advancing Law Firm Productivity through Modern ERP

A robust command center designed for legal professionals. It bridges the gap between complex legal workflows and efficient system automation, focusing on data integrity and high-performance backend architecture.

Case Lifecycle Hub

End-to-end management of lawsuits. Advanced filtering by status, signature, and priority ensures a clutter-free view of the active firm portfolio.

Smart DocGen Engine

Transform Word files into repeatable templates. Automatically map case-specific variables to document placeholders, reducing manual work by up to 90%.

Unified Client CRM

Secure database for individuals and corporate entities. Tracks contact details, PESEL, and associated case history in one persistent profile.

Product Tour

See It in Action

A guided walkthrough of the platform's core modules — from firm setup to document automation.

1

Organizational Structure

Build your firm's hierarchy from the ground up. Create departments, define roles, and manage every team member from a single, centralized dashboard. Everyone knows where they fit — and so does the system.

2

Effortless Case Management

Browse your entire caseload at a glance with powerful filtering and sorting. Attach DOCX and PDF files directly to any case and preview them instantly — no downloads, no context-switching, no friction.

3

Client Profiles, Simplified

Maintain a comprehensive, searchable database of every client. Contact details, identification numbers, and full case history — everything lives in one unified profile, always one click away.

4

Your Inbox, Built In

Access all your emails without ever leaving the platform. Sync your inbox from any major provider and keep every conversation tied to the right case and client — communication stays where the work happens.

5

Calendar & Scheduling

Stay on top of every hearing, meeting, and filing deadline. The integrated calendar keeps your entire practice organized and ensures nothing slips through the cracks.

6

Smart Document Templates

Turn repetitive paperwork into a one-click process. Upload any DOCX template, define your variables, and generate perfectly formatted documents filled with live case data — no word processor needed, ever.
